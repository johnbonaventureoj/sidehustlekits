The one trick behind every good prompt

Give the AI a role, context and a format. “Write an email” is weak; “You're my assistant. Reply to this landlord politely declining the rent rise, keep it under 120 words, friendly but firm” gets a usable answer first time.

Make it understand YOU first

Before anything else, paste a short “about me” so every later answer is tailored — your job, your tone, what you're trying to do. This single step transforms the quality of everything after it.
