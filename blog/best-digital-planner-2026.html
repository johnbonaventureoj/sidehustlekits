The Best Digital Planner for 2026 (Hyperlinked, Undated & Print-Ready)

How to choose a digital planner for 2026 that actually gets used — what hyperlinking, undated pages and GoodNotes support really mean, and a simple pick.

What makes a digital planner worth using?

Most planners get abandoned by February. The three things that keep a digital planner in daily use are simple: it must be hyperlinked (one tap to any page), undated (so a bad week doesn't waste printed dates), and it must work in the app you already use — usually GoodNotes, Notability or Samsung Notes.

Hyperlinked vs. scrolling

A hyperlinked planner turns your tablet into a real planner: tap a month, tap a week, tap “home” to get back. Without links you're endlessly scrolling, and that friction is exactly why planners get dropped.

Undated is the secret to actually finishing the year

Undated weekly and daily pages mean you start any time and reuse the planner every year. You never feel behind, and you never waste pages.
