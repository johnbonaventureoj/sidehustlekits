The ADHD Planner That Actually Works (No Guilt, No Broken Streaks)

Why most planners fail ADHD brains, and the small design changes — one daily priority, brain dumps, task triage — that make a planner you'll actually keep using.

Why normal planners fail ADHD brains

Standard planners assume you'll fill every box, keep every streak and remember to open them. For an ADHD brain that's a recipe for guilt and abandonment. The fix isn't more discipline — it's a planner designed around how the brain actually works.

One priority beats a long to-do list

Decision paralysis is real. A single “The ONE Thing” each day removes the overwhelm of choosing and gives you a clear win to aim for.

Brain dump, then triage

Getting the loud, swirling thoughts out of your head and onto the page is half the battle. A quick Brain Dump followed by Task Triage (now / soon / someday / drop) turns chaos into a short, doable list.
