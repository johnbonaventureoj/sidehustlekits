How to Build a Budget in 10 Minutes (Free Spreadsheet Method)

A dead-simple way to build a monthly budget fast — the three numbers that matter, and how to make it update itself so you only do the work once.

The only three numbers that matter

Forget 40 categories. Start with three: money in, fixed bills, and everything else. Once those balance, you can add detail. Most people over-complicate budgeting and quit in week one.

Make it update itself

The reason budgets fail is manual maths. Use a spreadsheet where you log spending once and the dashboard — savings rate, remaining budget, net worth — recalculates automatically. You do the typing; the sheet does the sums.

Excel or Google Sheets — either works

You don't need paid software. Google Sheets is free, and a good template's formulas carry over when you import an .xlsx.
